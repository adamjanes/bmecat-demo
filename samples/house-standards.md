# House standards — Rowan Fisk Lighting Design

Working notes. What we specify by default and why. Kept current-ish; override where the job
demands it, but say so.

---

## Brands we actually use

**ERCO** — the default for anything architectural. Photometrics are honest, the spec sheets are
complete, and the beam options are properly differentiated rather than three variations on
"flood". Lead times have been reliable. We use them for downlights, track, wallwashing, and
almost all gallery and retail work.

**Kreon** — where the fitting is going to be seen, or where the client cares about the
detailing. Cleaner apertures, better trimless and plaster-in options. More expensive; worth it
front-of-house. Their track system is good.

Rule of thumb: **ERCO for the light, Kreon for the look.** Where a fitting is visible in a
public area and the client has taste, price Kreon. Where it is doing a job in a ceiling nobody
looks at, ERCO.

## Standing defaults

| | Default | Notes |

| CCT — hospitality | **2700K** | Non-negotiable in bars, restaurants, hotel public areas. Clients ask for "warm" and mean 2700, not 3000. |
| CCT — retail | **3000K** | 2700 makes merchandise look muddy. |
| CCT — gallery / art | **3000K** | With CRI 95+. |
| CCT — office / back of house | **4000K** | |

## Things we always do

- **Recessed and trimless wherever the ceiling allows.** Most clients say they "hate seeing
  fittings" and they mean it. Surface-mounted is a fallback, not a starting point.
- **Wallwashing over downlighting for corridors.** A corridor lit by grazing the walls reads as
  wider and better finished than the same corridor with a row of downlights down the middle.
- **Separate circuits for accent vs ambient.** Always. Clients change their minds about levels
  after handover and this is what makes that cheap.
- **IP44 minimum in bathrooms**, IP65 in showers and anywhere external and unsheltered.
- **Emergency lighting is specified separately.** Do not mix it into the main schedule.

## Things we avoid

- Colour-changing anything unless the client has specifically asked and understands the
  commissioning cost.
- Integrated drivers in inaccessible ceilings — remote gear or it becomes a maintenance problem
  in year three.
- Specifying to the absolute lumen maximum of a fitting. Leaves no headroom and they run hot.
- More than about six fixture types in a single space. If the schedule needs more than that, the
  design is not resolved yet.

---

## Numbering

Types run `L01`, `L02`, … in the order they appear front-of-house to back-of-house, then
external. Emergency types get an `E` prefix on their own schedule.

Group rows by room. Clients read the schedule room by room, not type by type.
